The Billion-Dollar Disconnect (Problem & Solution)
Why traditional cybersecurity risk assessment fails Indian banks, and how OptiRisk delivers the quantitative standard required by modern regulatory frameworks.

❌ The Legacy Problem in Banks

  • Vague Heatmaps: Risk teams present arbitrary "Red, Amber, Green" matrices. A CFO cannot insure or budget against "Red".
  • CVE Alert Fatigue: Scanners flag 4,000+ CVEs. CISOs have no mathematical formula to know which flaw exposes the most dollars.
  • Blind Security Spending: Budgets are spent based on vendor marketing rather than mathematically proven risk reduction ROI.
  • Regulatory Penalty Risk: RBI, SEBI, and CERT-In demand empirical Cyber Risk Quantification and board-level reporting.

✓ The OptiRisk Breakthrough

  • Dollar-Denominated Loss: Translates technical CVEs and threat vectors into exact Expected Annual Loss ($ / ₹) with 95% confidence intervals.
  • Stochastic FAIR™ Modeling: Evaluates Threat Event Frequency (TEF), Vulnerability Exploitability, and Primary/Secondary Loss Magnitude.
  • Algorithmic Portfolio Optimizer: Employs constrained Knapsack optimization to allocate capital to controls that maximize Risk Reduction ROI.
  • Automated Regulatory Alignment: 100% mapped to RBI CSF, SEBI CSCRF, DPDP Act 2023, and NIST CSF 2.0.
